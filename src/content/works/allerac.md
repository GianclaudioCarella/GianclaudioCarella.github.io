Allerac is a self-hosted platform for AI agents. Each unit is an isolated instance, and a public website can offer a chat without exposing the rest of the platform.

I am the founder and work on it as an AI engineer. The public repository is [Allerac/allerac-one](https://github.com/Allerac/allerac-one).
